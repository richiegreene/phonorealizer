Phonorealism Perform
synchronised in-ear monitoring for spectral parts

I am playing

Choose your part, let the page hear your instrument, and follow the scrolling score. You will hear your own line, and as much of the rest of the ensemble as you want behind it.

I am conducting

Load a justidraw .sav or a phonorealizer CSV, group the partials into named parts, watch everyone's readiness and sync quality, then start the downbeat for the whole ensemble at once.
Nothing is streamed between devices. The hub distributes the score and a single timestamp for the downbeat; from there every device plays from its own clock.
